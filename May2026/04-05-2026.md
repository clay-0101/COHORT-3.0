## 📂 Basic Concepts: Files and Folders

- **File:** Ek single type ke data ka collection hota hai.
  - Format: `filename.filetype`
  - Examples: `notes.txt`, `photo.png`, `video.mp4`

- **Folder:** Multiple files ko ek saath contain karne ke liye use hota hai.

## 🌐 History of Internet & Web

### 1. The Background (1945 – 1950s)

- **Timeline:** 1945 se 1959 ke beech internet ki buniyad padne lagi thi.
- **World War Influence:** 1950s mein World War ke baad Axis (Germany, Japan, Italy) aur Alliance (USA, UK, USSR/Russia) ke beech tensions ne technology ko boost kiya.
- **Cold War:** USA aur USSR ke beech "Cold War" ki wajah se military communications ko secure karne ke liye network ki zaroorat padi.

## 🚀 Evolution of the Web (WWW)

**Tim Berners-Lee** ne Web ki duniya mein revolution laya aur teen main pillars diye:

1. **HTML** — HyperText Markup Language
2. **HTTP** — HyperText Transfer Protocol
3. **URL** — Uniform Resource Locator
